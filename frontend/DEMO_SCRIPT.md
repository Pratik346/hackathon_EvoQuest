# Life RPG — Demo Script (5 minutes)

1. **Landing page** — show the pitch, click "Begin Your Journey".
2. **Register** a fresh character — point out XP/Gold/attributes all start at 0.
3. **Dashboard** — show the character card and the AI Advisor panel.
4. **Quests** — create a quest ("Solve 2 DSA Problems", coding, medium).
5. **Complete the quest** — highlight the level-up overlay firing live, and the XP bar animating.
6. **Achievements** — show "First Flame" unlocked automatically, no manual trigger.
7. **Shop** — attempt to buy an item you can't afford (button disabled), then buy one you can.
8. **Inventory** — show the purchased item appearing immediately.
9. **Activity** — scroll the day-grouped timeline showing every event just performed.
10. **AI Advisor** — click "Analyze My Progress", show a real Gemini-backed recommendation.
11. **Anti-cheat callout** — double-click "Complete Quest" on an already-completed quest live, show the 409 gets silently handled with no double reward.

Close with: "Every reward — XP, Gold, Level, Achievements — is calculated and validated server-side. The frontend only ever *displays* state, never sets it."
